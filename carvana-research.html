Carvana profitability & valuation

October 2026

A company analysis connecting retail volumes, company profitability, financing activities, and capital structure.

Research question

How does growth in retail units translate into company profitability, and which operating and financing contributions need to be separated to understand that relationship?

Method

The workbook follows quarterly results from Q1 2023 through Q3 2026. I reconciled annual totals, compared the same quarter across years to reduce seasonal differences, and separated reported figures from calculated ratios.

The analysis also distinguishes retail vehicles, wholesale activities, and identified financing contributions. It does not allocate company EBITDA to individual businesses when that allocation is not disclosed.

Profitability comparison

Company adjusted EBITDA divided by retail units, in USD

Values are rounded from my October 2026 workbook. This ratio normalizes company profitability by retail volumes. It includes contributions beyond retail vehicle sales and is not profit attributable to an individual vehicle.

Valuation framework

I examined economic share counts, net debt, and the tax receivable agreement as separate inputs to the enterprise-to-equity value calculation. The framework keeps forward operating assumptions explicit, including retail units, EBITDA per retail unit, and the valuation multiple.

A historical calibration reproduces the observed market price. Forward scenarios require additional assumptions and are not presented as completed price targets.
