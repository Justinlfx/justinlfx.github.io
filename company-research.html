Netflix, Tesla & Nike

Business model and financial statement research

Three company profiles comparing how different business models generate revenue and translate customer demand into financial results.

Netflix

I examined the subscription streaming model, geographic revenue exposure, and the role of the content library and original productions in competitive positioning. The profile reviews revenue and earnings alongside the balance sheet to connect the business model with financial performance.

Tesla

I separated automotive sales, energy generation and storage, and services to understand the contribution of each revenue stream. The research also covers geographic exposure and the relationship between manufacturing, software, distribution, and competitive positioning.

Nike

I compared wholesale and direct-to-consumer distribution, product categories, and geographic revenue exposure. The profile considers brand strength, retail relationships, and scale alongside changes in revenue, earnings, and balance sheet items.

Comparison framework

Each profile follows the same structure: business model, geographic breakdown, competitive advantages, financial performance, and stock information. The purpose is to make the differences between business models clear before moving into valuation.
