Company analysis · October 2026

Carvana:
the economics of scale

A study of how retail volumes, company profitability, and financing activities fit together. I examined quarterly results, separated reported figures from derived ratios, and built a valuation framework around explicit assumptions.

Read research notesClose research notes

The workbook follows quarterly results from Q1 2023 through Q3 2026. It reconciles annual totals and compares the same quarter across years to reduce seasonal differences.

The key distinction is the denominator: company adjusted EBITDA includes more than retail vehicle sales. Dividing it by retail units creates a comparison ratio, not profit attributable to an individual vehicle.

The valuation framework separates economic share counts, net debt, and the tax receivable agreement. Forward operating assumptions remain inputs, so a historical calibration is not presented as a price target.
