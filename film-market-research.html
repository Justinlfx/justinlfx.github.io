Animated film market research

Independent research

2025

An independent research project examining the commercial performance of more than 50 animated films from Hollywood, Japan, and China.

Dataset and scope

The study considers release timing, seasonality, and competition in relation to box office revenue. It connects market conditions with the commercial performance of animated films across three production markets.

Analytical approach

I used regression and correlation analysis to assess relationships between release conditions and box office performance. I also considered narrative structures and audience reception using film theory, economics, and cultural studies.

Deliverables

I developed the research question, carried out the analysis, and presented the work in a 2,000-word paper, an academic poster, and an oral defense. The project brought quantitative analysis and industry context into a single research process.
